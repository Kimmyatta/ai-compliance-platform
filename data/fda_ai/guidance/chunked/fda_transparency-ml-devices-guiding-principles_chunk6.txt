What: Relevant information The type of information that is appropriate to share will vary across the range of MLMDs. It will depend on the benefits and risks of each MLMD and the needs of their intended users. It’s good practice to provide information that enhances understanding of the device and its intended use. A clear and accurate description of a device generally includes: • information about its medical purpose and function. • the diseases or conditions it’s meant to address. • intended users, use environments and target populations. It’s also good practice to explain how the device fits in the health care workflow. This would include a description of the intended inputs and outputs. It would also describe how the output is intended to impact health care decisions or actions, or the judgment of a health care professional.
