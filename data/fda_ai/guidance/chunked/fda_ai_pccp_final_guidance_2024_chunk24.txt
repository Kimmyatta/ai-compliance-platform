For example, section 515C provides that a supplemental application (section 515C(a)) or new 510(k) (section 515C(b)) is not required for a change to a device that would otherwise require a PMA supplement or a new 510(k) if the change is consistent with a PCCP approved or cleared by FDA. Section 515C also provides that FDA may require that a PCCP include labeling for safe and effective use of a device as such device changes pursuant to such plan, notification 4 Contains Nonbinding Recommendations requirements if the device does not function as intended pursuant to such plan, and performance requirements for changes made under the plan.12 While under the FD&C Act FDA may approve or clear a PCCP for a variety of devices, this guidance provides recommendations specifically for PCCPs for AI-DSFs.
