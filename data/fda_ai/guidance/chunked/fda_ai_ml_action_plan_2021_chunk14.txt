Additionally, there was a request for FDA to encourage harmonization of the numerous efforts to develop GMLP, including through consensus standards efforts, leveraging already-existing workstreams, and involvement of other communities focused on AI/ML. Given the need for GMLP, the Agency has been an active participant in numerous efforts related to their development, including standardization efforts and collaborative communities. For example, FDA maintains liaisons to the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) P2801 Artificial Intelligence Medical Device Working Group and the International Organization for Standardization/ Joint Technical Committee 1/ SubCommittee 42 (ISO/ IEC JTC 1/SC 42) - Artificial Intelligence; and it participates in the Association for the Advancement of Medical Instrumentation (AAMI)/ British Standards Institution (BSI) Initiative on AI in medical technology.
