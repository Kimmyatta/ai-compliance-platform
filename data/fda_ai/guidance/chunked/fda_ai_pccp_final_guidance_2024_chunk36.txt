This guidance is not intended to provide a complete description of what may be necessary to include in a marketing submission for an AI-DSF.32 The recommendations in this guidance do not change applicable statutory and regulatory standards, such as device clearance or approval standards, nor the applicable requirements, including marketing submission content requirements and the requirements for valid scientific evidence.33 FDA recommends that you refer to other guidances, as applicable to a specific device, for recommendations on aspects of the submission beyond the PCCP. This guidance is intended to provide recommendations on certain types of modifications that, at this time, FDA believes generally may be appropriate for inclusion in a PCCP for an AI-DSF. This guidance is not intended to delineate a comprehensive list of modifications FDA would consider appropriate for inclusion in a PCCP for an AI-DSF.
