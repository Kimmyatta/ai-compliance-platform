In addition to those submission types, for devices subject to 510(k) requirements, a special 510(k) submission may be appropriate to modify a PCCP where the modifications to a PCCP comprise changes to the manufacturer’s own device and PCCP and where well-established methods are available to evaluate the change to the PCCP.82 FDA intends to focus its review on the aspects of the device that are most significantly modified.83 For example, if the device has been relatively unchanged since FDA’s prior authorization and a modified PCCP is proposed, FDA would focus its review on the modified PCCP. Manufacturers may discuss proposed changes to the PCCP with the appropriate FDA review division through the Q-Submission Program. F.
