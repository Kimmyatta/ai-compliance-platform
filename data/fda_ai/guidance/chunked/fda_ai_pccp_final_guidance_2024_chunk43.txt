Predetermined Change Control Plan Predetermined Change Control Plan (PCCP): The documentation describing what modifications will be made to a device and how the modifications will be assessed. The modifications described in the PCCP include device modifications that would otherwise require a PMA supplement or new premarket notification.42 The PCCP includes a Description of Modifications, Modification Protocol, and Impact Assessment. PCCPs for AI-DSFs are the focus of this guidance.
