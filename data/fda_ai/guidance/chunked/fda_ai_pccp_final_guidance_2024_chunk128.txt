In certain circumstances, FDA may request additional Modification Protocol components or information to be included in a PCCP for some device types so that FDA can make a determination of reasonable assurance of safety and effectiveness or substantial equivalence when reviewing the device and PCCP.111 Additionally, some sections of a Modification Protocol may be more or less detailed depending on the complexity and risks of each modification in the PCCP. Overall, FDA recommends that manufacturers consider the topics and questions in this appendix for their particular AI-DSF and PCCP as a means of encouraging a detailed and methodically prepared PCCP.
