In light of the public health need to facilitate innovation for AI/ML-based devices while providing appropriate oversight for them, on January 12, 2021, the CDRH Digital Health Center of Excellence issued FDA’s Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning (AI/ML)-Based Software as a Medical Device Action Plan (“the Action Plan”). The Action Plan describes FDA’s strategy for addressing AI/ML-based devices in a holistic, collaborative, and multidisciplinary manner. An important pillar of the Action Plan is the further advancement of the tailored regulatory framework for AI/ML-based devices that was proposed in the 2019 discussion paper.
