The QSR also includes requirements to review and approve modifications to device design and production (21 CFR 820.30 and 820.70), and requirements to document changes and approvals in the device master record (21 CFR 820.181). 15 Contains Nonbinding Recommendations If the particular modification is consistent with the authorized PCCP, a new marketing submission is not necessary; the modification can be implemented in accordance with the Modification Protocol, and the manufacturer should document that modification and the analysis in accordance with the manufacturer’s quality system.
