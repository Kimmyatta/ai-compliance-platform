Impact Assessment documentation for a PCCP in a marketing submission should discuss how the individual modifications included in the PCCP impact not only the AI-DSF, but also how they impact the overall functionality of the device, including how they impact other device software functions and/or device hardware. For combination products, such documentation should also discuss how the individual modifications included in the PCCP for the device constituent part impact the biologic and/or drug constituent part, and the combination product as a whole.
