These recommendations are based on the statutory authorities provided in the FD&C Act, including the provisions added by FDORA, as well as feedback obtained through our various interactions with interested parties. III. Scope This guidance is applicable to AI-DSFs that the manufacturer intends to modify over time. This includes AI-DSFs for which modifications to the AI model are implemented automatically (i.e., for which the modifications are implemented automatically by software, also known as “continuous learning”), AI-DSFs for which modifications to the AI model are implemented manually (i.e., involving steps that require human input, action, review, and/or decision-making, and therefore are not implemented automatically), or a combination of both.
