Regulatory Science Methods Related to Algorithm Bias & Robustness What we heard: Stakeholders described the need for improved methods to evaluate and address algorithmic bias and to promote algorithm robustness. What we’ll do: Support regulatory science efforts to develop methodology for the evaluation and improvement of machine learning algorithms, including for the identification and elimination of bias, and for the evaluation and promotion of algorithm robustness. Bias and generalizability is not an issue exclusive to AI/ML-based devices.
