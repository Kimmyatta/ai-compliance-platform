Introduction FDA has a longstanding commitment to develop and apply innovative approaches to the regulation of medical device software and other digital health technologies to ensure their safety and effectiveness.1 As technology continues to advance all facets of healthcare, medical software incorporating artificial intelligence (AI), including the subset of AI known as machine learning (ML), has become an important part of many medical devices. This guidance is intended to provide a forward-thinking approach to promote the development of safe and effective AI- enabled devices.
