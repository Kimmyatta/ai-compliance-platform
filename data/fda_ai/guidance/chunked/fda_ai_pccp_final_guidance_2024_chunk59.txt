For 510(k) submissions, in making a determination of substantial equivalence where the predicate device was authorized with a PCCP, the subject device must be compared to the version of the predicate device cleared or approved prior to changes made under the PCCP.57 Once a 510(k) for a device that includes modifications that have been implemented consistent with the authorized PCCP has been cleared in a subsequent marketing submission, such device can now serve as an eligible predicate device.
