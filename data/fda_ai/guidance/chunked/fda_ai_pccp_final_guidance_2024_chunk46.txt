Manufacturers should evaluate the impact of modifications to their devices and must generally submit a marketing submission when device modifications affect the intended use of the device or could significantly affect the safety or effectiveness of the device.43 An authorized PCCP specifies planned modifications that, if not included in a PCCP, could otherwise require a new marketing submission pursuant to 21 CFR 807.81(a)(3) and 21 CFR 814.39(a), and consistent with the Device Modifications guidances. An authorized PCCP should include the following sections, which will be further described in Sections VI. through VIII. of this guidance: • Description of Modifications (Section VI.) – The specifications for the characteristics and performance of the planned modifications to the device; • Modification Protocol (Section VII.)
